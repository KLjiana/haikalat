# 0.28.1 S1 接触阴影技术卡：主方向光短距离接触遮挡

日期：2026-11-11（2026-11-12 修订：见文末生产屏幕空间 pass）。状态：生产屏幕空间实现，JVM 与真实 GL 验收见文末。对应路线图 #9
（Contact Shadows / S1），执行计划见 [multi-agent full delivery](haikalat-multi-agent-full-delivery.md)，
技术列表见 [realtime-rendering-roadmap](realtime-rendering-roadmap.md)。

## 范围与归属

- 归属：`render3d.shadow` 的配置与 `render3d.lighting.FrameLightBinder` 的上传职责；
  消费端是现有唯一 `pbr-forward.frag`，不新增 manager、factory、Pass 或 GPU 资源。
- 首版只处理一个主方向光（`uDirectionalShadowFrameLightIndex` 指向的已选阴影灯）。
  点光、聚光、无方向光阴影、非 PBR 材质明确不消费；启用但无方向光阴影或 PBR 时由
  `RenderFeaturePolicy` 拒绝请求，而不是静默忽略。
- 默认关闭。`ShadowSettings` 原四参数/五参数构造器保留，`contactShadows()` 为
  `ContactShadowSettings.disabled()`；因此既有场景和默认输出不变。

## 显式控制

| 控制 | 默认 | 合法范围 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `false` | — | 总开关；`active() = enabled && strength > 0` |
| `distance` | `1.0` | `(0, 10]` 世界单位 | 朝光源的最大步进距离 |
| `thickness` | `0.5` | `(0, distance]` 世界单位 | 阴影图深度容差/接触带厚度 |
| `steps` | `12` | `[1, 32]` | 每帧每片元的采样上限 |
| `strength` | `0.8` | `[0, 1]` | 直射光最大衰减比例 |

`ContactShadowSettings` 校验有限性、范围和 `thickness <= distance`；`steps` 在 shader 内再次
`clamp` 到 `CONTACT_SHADOW_MAX_STEPS`，因此即使宿主上传异常值也不会突破 32 次采样。

## 输入、输出与合成

- 输入：共享的 `vViewDepth`（级联选择）、级联/单张方向光 shadow map 及其矩阵、
  `uShadowBias`、接收面世界位置/法线、`uStableViewProjection`（屏幕边缘回退）。
- 输出：只调制该主方向光的 `visibility`；IBL、emissive、环境背景和其他灯不受影响。
- 先算现有级联可见度 `visibility = 1 - directionalShadowFactor`。接触项为
  `contact = directionalContactShadow * visibility`，再
  `visibility = max(visibility - contact, 0)`。已经被级联完全遮蔽的像素接触项为 0，
  半影区按剩余可见度缩放，避免对已有 cascaded shadow 二次压暗。
- 接触项在接收面越出级联 tile、位于相机后方或屏幕边缘 5% 内时返回 0；这正是离屏/边缘
  回退：回退到现有 cascaded 结果，而不是伪造完整阴影。

## 算法与有界性

对当前级联，从 `worldPosition + normalBias + l * startOffset` 起沿 `l`（指向光源）步进
`distance * step / steps`，投影回同一级联的 shadow-map 深度，和存储深度比较：

- `separation = projected.z - storedDepth`；
- `separation > bias` 表示射线点后方（更靠近光源）存在遮挡；
- `separation <= bias + thickness * depthPerWorld` 把贡献限制在接触带内。

命中即取 `1 - 0.65 * step/steps` 并停止；最多 32 次 shadow-map 采样。该实现是
**shadow-map 空间**的短距离 ray march：它消费现有深度/shadows 约定，但不采样当前帧的
屏幕深度附件。真正的屏幕空间深度 ray march 需要几何 pass 之前的 mask pass；本轮 worker
约束禁止修改 `RenderPipeline`，也无法在不新增 pass 的情况下读取 PBR 正在写入的深度附件，
因此该扩展留待后续集成，不在本卡宣称完成。

## 资源生命周期与成本

- 无新纹理、FBO、shader 或 Pass；`ContactShadowSettings` 是纯值对象，随
  `ShadowSettings`/`RenderFeatureSettings` 发布。
- `FrameLightBinder` 每次材质/帧状态绑定时上传 5 个标量 uniform；关闭时
  `uContactShadowEnabled=0`，`directionalContactShadow` 在第一次采样前返回，图像精确不变。
- 成本：每条被主方向光照亮且未完全处于级联阴影中的 PBR 片元最多 32 次 shadow-map
  采样；默认 12 次，`steps` 与 `distance` 均有上限。
- 修改设置走现有 `RenderFeaturesControl` 批次/代次发布；首版不提供逐帧热改，避免绕过
  `RenderPipeline` 的 frame-boundary 事务。

## Scope revision: production screen-space pass (2026-11-12)

Status: the shadow-map-space short march above is retained only as a material-level forward option. #9 now has a real screen-space depth consumer wired into the production `RenderPipeline`.

### Inputs, output and ownership

- New internal `com.kaleblangley.haikalat.subsystems.render3d.shadow.ScreenSpaceContactShadowPass` owns one full-size single-sample RGBA16F colour target, one shader pair and one full-screen mesh.
- It borrows the graph's scene colour and the shared single-sample camera scene depth (`topology.sceneSurface().single(SceneBufferChannel.DEPTH)`); it never writes depth and never samples a target it is writing.
- Recording first resolves the scene colour into the pass target, then draws a full-screen multiplicative darkening `target *= 1 - strength * occlusion` (`blendFunc(GL_ZERO, GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_COLOR)`, alpha preserved) and the pipeline blits the result back into the shared colour chain.
- The constructor rejects inactive settings and `samples > 1` with a clear message; inactive settings create no pass, target, shader, mesh or draw. `resize` allocates a complete candidate before publishing, and `close` is idempotent and aggregates failures.

### Shader algorithm

- Reconstruct the view-space position from the single-sample depth with `uInverseProjection` (the jittered projection that produced the frame).
- Derive a geometric normal with `cross(dFdx(viewPosition), dFdy(viewPosition))`, orient it toward the eye and reject receivers facing away from the light (back-facing self-shadow taps).
- March toward the light in view space from a small normal offset, at most `ContactShadowSettings.steps()` (clamped to 32) taps over `distance`.
- Project each tap with the jittered `uProjection`. Taps outside `[0,1]` are skipped, never clamped: a receiver whose ray leaves the raster falls back to no occlusion.
- An in-raster tap occludes when `rayDepth - sampledSurfaceDepth > bias` and the separation is within `thickness`. The first hit stops the march and is weighted by the travelled fraction.

### Production wiring and gating

- `PipelineTopology` adds the internal `screenSpaceContactShadows` component and `screenSpaceContactShadowCompositionActive(settings, contactShadows, directionalLightPresent)`. The key requires `ContactShadowSettings.active()`, HDR, a non-MSAA (single-sample) raster and a present directional light. The pass also requests the shared single-sample depth structurally.
- `PipelineGeneration` owns/borrows the pass with the caustics lifecycle: `assembleScreenSpaceContactShadows(...)`, resize candidate, close/retire, and a test accessor.
- `RenderPipeline` declares the pass after the current colour producer and before water/refraction/OIT/stochastic consumers (immediately after caustics in the graph), depends on the depth producer, and sets `postProcess.sceneColorInput(...)`. The graph pass shares the scene colour in place and does not attach a depth attachment.
- `PipelineFeaturePolicy` fails closed when a hand-built topology claims the pass without HDR, a single-sample target or a single-sample depth surface.

### Limits

- Single directional light only (the first directional light, matching the caustics/light scheduler convention).
- Single-sample raster only; MSAA is reported unavailable and leaves the graph unchanged. The pass is projected with the current jittered matrices, so both NONE and TAA rasters are supported.
- Screen-space visibility only: an occluder absent from the camera depth buffer (hidden behind the visible surface) casts no contact shadow.
- No temporal accumulation, denoising or variable penumbra; one bounded march, at most 32 taps.
- With volumetric fog/particles, the current insertion point is after that transparent replay, so the composite can also attenuate the fogged colour. Water, refraction, OIT and stochastic transparency consume it (`waterDependencies` and `sceneColorInput` ordering).
- Enabling the request still passes the existing `RenderFeaturePolicy` contact-shadow admission (enabled directional shadows with a shadow-casting directional light and at least one PBR material), because the same settings continue to drive the material-level forward term. The screen-space pass itself only needs HDR, a single-sample raster and a directional light; a future revision can relax the admission when the forward term is retired.

## Combination verification (2026-11-16, worktree `codex/render-contact-combos`)

Closes the #9 review gaps for TAA jitter, resize, water/OIT, multi-light first-only behaviour and derivative-normal silhouettes. Two new real-GL classes were added and registered in `gradle/focused-verification.gradle`:

- `ScreenSpaceContactShadowTaaResizeGlTest` (2 cases)
- `ScreenSpaceContactShadowCompositionGlTest` (3 cases, five fixtures including water-only, OIT-only and water+OIT)

### Multi-light production fix

`RenderPipeline.screenSpaceContactShadowExecutor` previously passed `firstDirectionalLight()` to the pass. The forward material term applies the contact factor only to `uDirectionalShadowFrameLightIndex`, so a scene whose scheduler selected a later directional light marched in the wrong direction. A two-light probe with a priority-1 selected light measured selected-side delta `0.0` and opposite-side delta `0.1126` before the fix. The executor now resolves `shadowFramePlan().directional().entry().light()` and fails closed when no directional shadow light is selected. After the fix the measured selected side is `0.1482` and the opposite side `0.0`.

### Fixtures and measured values

- **TAA**: static camera, 0.4 x 1.5 occluder on a 12 x 12 floor, `AntiAliasingMode.TAA`, eight frames; frames 4-7 cover the four jitter phases. Stable NONE reference centroid `(83.75, 52.34)` with total darkening `9.216`. Maximum pre-resolve centroid offset `0.89 px`; maximum TAA-resolved offset `0.72 px`; maximum pairwise resolved spread `0.92 px`; total darkening stayed within 15% across frames. The counterfactual is therefore sub-texel: a stable projection would place the darkening at the same centroid within `0.89 px`; a whole-texel shift was not observable through the public API at the pass's 0.25 px jitter, while the per-frame subpixel motion is observed and bounded.
- **Resize**: pipeline path 160x120 -> 200x160 -> 160x120 with the window and both baseline/contact pipelines resized. Graph and contact-pass descriptions reported `160x120`, `200x160`, `160x120`; the strongest floor darkening was `0.1668` at all three extents, `5.4 / 6.0 / 5.4 px` from the projected occluder base, and tracked live GL resources stayed at `110` before and after each resize. No GL error and no leak.
- **Water / OIT**: the contact receiver is composited before water and weighted OIT. Contact-channel luminance before -> after: water `0.15765 -> 0.05395` (delta `0.10370`), OIT `0.14593 -> 0.04871` (delta `0.09722`), water+OIT `0.13556 -> 0.05778` (delta `0.07779`). Disabling contact restored the byte-identical baseline in all three cases. Execution order was `ScreenSpaceContactShadowPass < WaterSurfacePass < WeightedOitPass`; the OIT pass still had `WaterSurfacePass` in `directDependencies`, so the contact pass did not change the transparent-collector order.
- **Multi-light**: priority-1 light selected as `frameLightIndex=1`; the first light was rejected as `LOWER_PRIORITY`. Selected-side floor delta `0.14824`; opposite-side floor delta `0.0`; the +x-facing PBR panel lit only by the non-selected light had delta `0.0` (independent check: `n dot toOther = 0.98`, `n dot toSelected = -0.98`). Only 286 pixels darkened (1.5% of the frame); no pixel brightened; disabling contact restored the exact baseline.
- **Derivative-normal silhouettes**: 0.22 x-sloped receiver with a 0.35 x 1.5 thin occluder and a near-vertical light. A floor pixel adjacent to the occluder silhouette whose analytic light ray misses the occluder (and misses the shadow band) had delta `0.0`; the sloped receiver still darkened at the contact probe by `0.06264`; total darkened pixels 65 (0.34%), so the isolated occluder did not produce full-screen darkening. The pixel-adjacent probe is selected by baseline colour adjacency plus an independent ray/AABB check, not by reading the pass output.

### Fixture conformance note

The existing `ScreenSpaceContactShadowsGlTest` floor vertex shader and the new fixtures now compute `world = uModel * position` and `gl_Position = uProjection * uView * world`. The shared surface prepass and forward shaders must produce the same clip-space value for `GL_LEQUAL` redraw; the old floor shader multiplied `uProjection * uView * uModel * vec4` directly and could lose the floor colour by one depth ULP when the contact feature added the shared-depth prepass. No assertion was relaxed: the existing 2 cases and `ContactShadowsGlTest` still pass unchanged in intent.

### Remaining limits (NOT_EVALUATED)

- The screen-space composite multiplies total scene colour. The forward material term is per selected light, but the production pass has no per-light colour decomposition; pixels lit by both directional lights are attenuated as a whole. The panel case proves a second-light-only surface is untouched; mixed-light attenuation is not decomposed per light.
- Derivative normal is still a 2x2 screen derivative approximation. The tested silhouette probe leaks zero, but no exhaustive scan of arbitrary depth discontinuities/edges is claimed.
- Multi-light coverage is the selected directional shadow light only; local lights, point/spot shadows and more than two directionals are not covered.
- MSAA is rejected by the pass (`samples > 1`); TAA is covered, TAA+resize is not.
- Water waves were static in the transparent-collector cases so the disabled baseline could be byte-identical; moving water with contact shadows is not pixel-tested.
- Performance, memory, human visual quality and multi-GPU are NOT_EVALUATED.
